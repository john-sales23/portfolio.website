Viva Energy Drink

Rationale

  • Adobe Photoshop 2024
  • Adobe Illustrator 2024

  • Canva

A group project to simulate working with other individuals in the graphic design field. The project was designed to develop a competitve marketing strategy by targeting specific demographics and creating distint visual design. Each group was tasked with creating a brand that is based on their research of targeted audience. I was tasked with doing quantative and qualitative research and assisted in conceptualizing logo designs. I also assisted in creating drink mockups and did quality control of final designs.
