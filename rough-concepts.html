Rough Concepts

Preface

  • Adobe Photoshop 2024
  • Procreate
  • Clip Studio Paint Pro

Rough concepts and sketches mainly created using iPad Pro 11 2024. I make character designs and colour palettes based on said design as hobby, ideas mostly generated from moodboards and music playlist. The concepts take roughly 1 hour for sketches, 30 minutes to create line art, another 30 minutes for making values, and another 30 minutes to apply colour. I often make corrections to colour and line art after the fact, usually within 2-3 days of not looking at it. I use these as an exercise to develop better colour and value accuracy as well as practice creating recognizable character silhouettes.
